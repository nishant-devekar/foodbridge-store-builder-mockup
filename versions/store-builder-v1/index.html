Store Builder — Discovery Prototype (wiring hub)

Iteration goal: a distributor builds his FoodBridge store in about 10 minutes — on a computer or a phone — without typing his customers, and sends it to FoodBridge's customer success team in one click.

Screens

  • Shop — entry: mobile (required), GST, business type, warehouses
  • Contacts — bring them in: phone QR (primary), iCloud, Tally/Excel list, paste
  • Who is who? — mark each contact Customer / Supplier / Staff, or remove
  • Daily work — six yes/no and choice questions, a note (speak or type)
  • Files — paper by the phone's camera, files by drag-and-drop, a voice note
  • Send — the summary, what is still missing, Build my store
  • Phone page from the QR (photo mode) — on his phone; Contacts and Files open it for their live QR
  • What reached FoodBridge — every build sent from this browser, its summary and files as they arrived
  • The phone layout — the same flow under 1000 px; one address flips by screen width

Decisions this iteration carries (owner, 2026-09-26 … 10-01)

StepsShop · Contacts · Daily work · Files · Send. Products and Warehouse stock are hidden, not deleted (model.js HIDDEN_STEPS).
ContactsOne marking step (Customer / Supplier / Staff). Delivery days, areas, terms and staff jobs are hidden questions (HIDDEN_ASKS).
FilesEvery file reaches the team as it came (raw/); over 2.5 MB it travels in pieces.
iPhoneSafari's one-time Contact Picker setting first; iCloud on the computer second.
WordsTitles only, no sub-text; one action per screen; Hindi and English.

How it runs with no server

The prototype files under screens/prototype/ are byte-for-byte foodbridge-mock-platform v7/store-builder @ 3c840e6. Where it would call the bridge (/api/stores, /api/handoff), a service worker — discovery-bridge.js — answers instead and keeps everything in this browser. No request leaves the browser. Serve over HTTP (a service worker does not run from file://). The phone page gets a stand-in contact picker on a desktop browser.

Owner-side data: seed-data/seed.json (the prototype's own sample answers; in the prototype: “Try it with sample data”). The team's side is the customer-success module's own discovery.
