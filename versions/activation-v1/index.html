Store Activation — Discovery Prototype (wiring hub)

Working copy: v1 (frozen in versions/activation-v1/) plus addenda 003–023 — the earlier chat on the store link, titles only on Getting started, and step 1 redesigned: four ways in, one flow (choose → processing → check & save) and the products sheet.

Iteration goal: can a new owner who has never seen FoodBridge get from the store link to running their business (items, customers, first order, delivery, payment, daily plan) on one page, with the floating assistant only pointing the way? Everything works end to end in this browser; progress is kept in localStorage.

The floating assistant (bottom-right) is on Getting started, All set and the dashboard. Tap it and ask "how do I deliver?": it answers with a pointer to the page and never runs a step.

Discovery aids

  • Finish processing now (files and photos take 6 seconds, the other ways 3–4)
  • Put the sample rate list in the sheet now (14 rows, 5 to fix, 1 with no rate)
  • Start over (clears this browser's progress)

An uploaded file or photo is not read: the seed's rate list stands in for files and photos. Speaking uses the browser's own speech recognition where it has one (Chrome sends the audio to its speech service); elsewhere the mic types an example. No message is sent anywhere; WhatsApp screens show what each person would see.

One real call: Search products asks Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org, India only, food only) as you type — the search words leave the browser. Live results: Open Food Facts contributors, ODbL. If the service is busy or offline, FoodBridge's own list still answers.

The sheet works like Excel or Google Sheets: tap or click a cell, type to replace it, Enter moves down, Tab moves right, arrows move, Delete clears, Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes. On a phone, tap a cell twice to edit it in the formula bar.
