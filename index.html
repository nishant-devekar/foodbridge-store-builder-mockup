Store Builder — discovery

Two frozen discovery versions of the FoodBridge Store Builder module. Each stands on its own: no server, no real API call, invented data. In the product they share one database, and the team reads builds through Store Builder's published contract, never its tables.

Owner → team, joined

Both versions side by side: build a store on the left, and it arrives on the right as a New ticket — read through Store Builder's contract, never its storage.

Team key: fb-team-demo. On a phone: Owner and Team tabs.

store-builder-v3

The owner builds his store on a computer or a phone: Shop · Contacts · Who is who · Daily work · Files · Send. Each screen follows the screen it is opened on: on a phone, the phone layout. The phone QR, Build my store and “What reached FoodBridge” all work in this browser.

On a computer, “Add from phone” (phone layout) and the QR phone page pick six invented contacts. On the desktop Contacts and Files screens, use “Open the phone page for this QR”. Each browser keeps its own builds, so a real phone scanning the QR is not part of this demo.
