Store Builder — discovery

Discovery versions of the FoodBridge Store Builder modules. Each stands on its own: no server, no real API call, invented data. In the product they share one database, and the team reads builds through Store Builder's published contract, never its tables.

FoodBridge Assistant — assistant-v6 (new, 5 Oct 2026 — for review)

The owner's second round: mobile first, then the shop's name (no owner's name); business, returns and mornings take as many answers as apply; "anything else?" after contacts, products and papers; every message cut to one line. It stops at the store request: "Done! We'll set up your store and send you a login link."

Share this link: the Assistant · also inside the Control Tower · the discovery hub. Give feedback (bottom of the chat list on a computer, or ⋯ on a phone) sends a rating and a comment to the FoodBridge team — the one thing here that leaves the browser.

FoodBridge Assistant — assistant-v5 (superseded by v6)

Store Builder as a WhatsApp-style chat: say hi, and the Assistant sets up the store one question at a time — name, mobile, contacts, who is who, the business, how the day runs, products read from a rate list — then sums it up, creates the store, and offers what to do next. On a computer it looks like WhatsApp Web; on a phone, like WhatsApp.

Share this link: the Assistant · also inside the Control Tower · the discovery hub (the question tree and decisions). Give feedback (bottom of the chat list on a computer, or ⋯ on a phone) sends a rating and a comment to the FoodBridge team — the one thing here that leaves the browser. Everything else is invented data in this browser; a real phone scanning the QR code is not part of this demo (use the link beside the code).

store-builder-v4

The owner builds his store on a computer or a phone: Shop · Contacts · Who is who · Daily work · Files · Send. Each screen follows the screen it is opened on: on a phone, the phone layout. The phone QR, Build my store and “What reached FoodBridge” all work in this browser.

The FoodBridge online / down switch in each top bar shows what happens when FoodBridge can't be reached. On a computer, “Add from phone” and the QR phone page pick six invented contacts; an iPhone shows Safari's one-time setting, as the real page does. On the desktop Contacts and Files screens, use “Open the phone page for this QR”. Each browser keeps its own builds, so a real phone scanning the QR is not part of this demo.
